# ✈️ Beyond the Runway

### Aviation-Inspired Quotes About Life, Ambition, and Perseverance

A collection of aviation-inspired quotes connecting flight, ambition, challenges, and the journey toward our goals.

> *The journey begins before the sky does.*

---

## 1. Takeoff, Destination & Success

### 1. A delayed takeoff is not a cancelled flight.
**Türkçe:** Geciken kalkış, iptal edilen uçuş değildir.

A delay does not mean the destination is lost. Sometimes, patience is part of the journey.

### 2. No takeoff, no destination.
**Türkçe:** Kalkış yoksa varış da yok.

You must take the first step before you can reach your goal.

### 3. The sky is not the limit; it's the beginning.
**Türkçe:** Gökyüzü sınır değil, başlangıçtır.

### 4. Every flight begins with a decision to rise.
**Türkçe:** Her uçuş, yükselmeye karar vermekle başlar.

### 5. You were not born to stay on the ground.
**Türkçe:** Yerde kalmak için doğmadın.

### 6. No flight begins in the sky.
**Türkçe:** Hiçbir uçuş gökyüzünde başlamaz.

### 7. You can't reach the destination without leaving the gate.
**Türkçe:** Kapıdan ayrılmadan varış noktasına ulaşamazsın.

### 8. Every destination requires a departure.
**Türkçe:** Her varış noktası bir kalkış gerektirir.

### 9. No departure, no arrival.
**Türkçe:** Kalkış yoksa varış da yok.

### 10. The longest journey still starts with takeoff.
**Türkçe:** En uzun yolculuk bile kalkışla başlar.

### 11. No attempt, no achievement.
**Türkçe:** Deneme yoksa başarı da yok.

## About This Project

**Beyond the Runway** is a personal collection of original and aviation-inspired sayings about ambition, patience, courage, resilience, and pursuing goals.

The quotes are presented in English with Turkish translations. They are intended for inspiration, creative projects, social media, and aviation-themed content.

Some sayings may resemble common expressions or familiar motivational ideas. No famous authorship is claimed.
